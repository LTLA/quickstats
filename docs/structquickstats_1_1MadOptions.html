◆ difference_between_infinities_is_zero

template<typename Output_ = double>
bool quickstats::MadOptions< Output_ >::difference_between_infinities_is_zero = false

Assume that all occurrences of infinity of the same sign refer to the same arbitrarily large number, such that a value of infinity has a deviation of zero from a median of infinity (instead of NaN via usual IEEE arithmetic). This ensures that the MAD will be well-defined for a non-empty array, i.e., either 0 or infinity.
